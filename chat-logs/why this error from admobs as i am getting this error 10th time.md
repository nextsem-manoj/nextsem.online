### 🚨 Top 6 Reasons for Repeated AdMob Account Rejection

#### 1. Duplicate Account (Reason #1 for 90% of repeated rejections)
* **The Issue:** Google strictly allows **only ONE AdSense/AdMob account per person/entity**.
* **Why it fails:** If you ever created an AdSense account, YouTube monetization account, or another AdMob account using a different email address but with the same **phone number, name, payment details, or IP address**, Google will automatically reject all new sign-ups.
* **How to Fix:** 
  1. Search all your Google accounts for an old AdSense or AdMob email from Google.
  2. Either **cancel/close the old account** via AdSense settings OR log into AdMob using the original Google account.

---

#### 2. App Not Published on an Official App Store
* **The Issue:** AdMob requires your mobile app to be live and published on an officially supported store:
  * 🟢 **Google Play Store**
  * 🍏 **Apple App Store**
  * 📦 **Amazon Appstore** or Samsung Galaxy Store
* **Why it fails:** If your app is still in draft mode, uploaded only as an `.apk` on Google Drive/website, or in internal/testing tracks, AdMob cannot perform app store verification.
* **Note for Websites:** If you are trying to monetize a website (`.com` / `.online`), **you must use Google AdSense, NOT AdMob**. AdMob is strictly for native mobile apps.

---

#### 3. Missing or Invalid Privacy Policy Link
* **The Issue:** AdMob program policies require your app to have an active Privacy Policy URL.
* **Why it fails:**
  * The Privacy Policy link on your **Google Play Console store listing** is broken or gives a 404 error.
  * The Privacy Policy does not explicitly state that you use **Google AdMob / Advertising Identifiers (GAID / IDFA)** to serve ads.

---

#### 4. Low Quality / WebView Wrapper / Insufficient Content
* **The Issue:** Google rejects apps that provide minimal user value.
* **Why it fails:**
  * The app is just a basic website wrapped in a WebView without native app functionality.
  * The app has non-functional buttons, broken screens, or placeholder content.
  * Copyright issues (e.g., using trademarked brand names, logos, or unauthorized content).

---

#### 5. Using Live Ad Units During Development
* **The Issue:** Testing live AdMob Ad Unit IDs inside your app during development before account approval triggers Google's automated **Invalid Traffic / Policy Enforcement** filter.
* **How to Fix:** Always use Google's official **Test Ad Unit IDs** inside your code until your account and app are fully approved.

---

#### 6. Incomplete Payment & Identity Details
* **The Issue:** Your Google Pay / Payments Profile attached to the email address has missing tax info, incomplete address details, or an unverified name.

---

### 📋 Action Plan to Fix & Get Approved

1. **Check Your Gmail Inbox:** Look for an email from `admob-noreply@google.com` or `payments-noreply@google.com`. It will state the exact policy reason (e.g., *"You already have an existing account"* or *"App store verification failed"*).
2. **Resolve Duplicate Accounts:** If you have an old AdSense account, close it at [adsense.google.com](https://adsense.google.com) `Account > Settings > Account Information > Close Account`.
3. **Verify Play Console / App Store Listing:** Ensure your app is published on the Play Store/App Store with a live Privacy Policy URL.
4. **Use Web AdSense for Web Apps:** If your platform is a web app, sign up at [adsense.google.com](https://adsense.google.com) instead of AdMob.
5. **Re-submit:** Once the above is fixed, check the box *"I confirm I've read and am meeting AdMob Program Policies"* and click **Resubmit**.
