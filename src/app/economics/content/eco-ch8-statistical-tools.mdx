# Chapter 8: Use of Statistical Tools

## 📋 Steps in a Statistical Project

To solve a real-world economic question, researchers conduct statistical projects following five systematic steps:

1. **❓ Identifying the Problem:** Define the target question (e.g., "Why is local traffic congestion increasing?").
2. **📝 Designing a Questionnaire:** Draft a list of clear, short, and unbiased questions to collect data.
3. **📂 Collection of Data:** Distribute the questionnaire to a sample population via online forms or physical interviews.
4. **📊 Classification & Presentation:** Organise the raw responses into tables and visualize them using bar charts or pie diagrams.
5. **🧮 Analysis & Interpretation:** Calculate measures like Mean, Median, or Correlation to draw logical conclusions.

---

## 📑 Writing the Project Report

A professional project report contains the following standard structure:

* **Title Page:** Project title, names of investigators, and dates.
* **Introduction:** Scope and objectives of the study.
* **Methodology:** Sampling technique used and population size.
* **Analysis & Key Findings:** Tables, graphs, and calculations.
* **Conclusion & Recommendations:** Actionable advice based on findings.

> 📝 **Tip:** Keep questionnaire questions short, simple, and avoid asking personal, sensitive, or offensive details.

## 🏆 Concept Check

### ❓ Quick Question:
Which of the following represents the correct first step when starting a statistical project?

* **[A]** Drawing a pie chart of survey results
* **[B]** Writing the final project report
* **[C]** Identifying the problem or research objective
* **[D]** Calculating the Karl Pearson correlation coefficient

**Correct Answer:** **C** (Identifying the problem - you must define your research objective before designing surveys or performing statistical calculations).
