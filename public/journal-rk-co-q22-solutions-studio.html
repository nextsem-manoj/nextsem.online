💡 Golden Principles Applied in Q22:

  • Trade Discount Deduction: Trade discount is deducted immediately from the catalogue list price. It never appears in the debit or credit money columns of the journal.
  • Return of Discounted Goods (Txn v & xii): When goods bought/sold at a trade discount are returned, the trade discount percentage must be deducted from their list price as well.
  • Cheque Payments (Txn i & vi): Cheque issued is credited to Bank A/c. Cheque received and deposited is debited to Bank A/c.
  • Cost Plus Profit (Txn x & xi): Profit percentage is added to Cost first to establish catalogue selling price, then trade discount is subtracted from this sum.
