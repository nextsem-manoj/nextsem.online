📄 Page 2: Let us Think (Comprehension Questions)

Q1. What is Papa searching for?
Answer: Papa is searching for his lost spectacles (glasses), which was his only pair.
Q2. What are the possible places where Papa can find it?
Answer: Papa checked inside his pockets, glanced under his chair, and searched near the window.
Q3. At last, where does Papa find his lost item?
Answer: At last, Papa finds his spectacles right on top of his head!

💭 Page 2 & 3: Think and Discuss

1. Apart from reading, guess some other chores that Papa cannot do without the spectacles.
Answer: Without his spectacles, Papa cannot watch television, chop or cut vegetables safely, drive a car or scooter, thread a needle, or read small print on medicine bottles.
2. What makes your papa happy?
Answer: Spending quality time with family, seeing his children study well and be kind, drinking a warm cup of evening tea, and finding his misplaced belongings quickly make Papa happy!

📖 Page 6: Prepositions in Story Context

Options: around, after, before, in, above, on, among

My father has a habit of keeping his spectacles in random places. Last week, he could not find his spectacles and looked around the house. We checked the big box that reached above our waists. My father looked for the spectacles among the newspapers kept on the table. He continued searching for two hours before finally giving up. He sat down on the bed after giving one final look to the room and said with a sigh, ‘Looks like it is time to get a new pair.’

✍️ Page 8: Convert Poem Into a Story

Today, my father is unhappy because he has misplaced his spectacles, and it was his only pair. Without them, he is helpless—he cannot read the morning newspaper, watch his favorite TV show, or help Mother cut the vegetables.

He searched in distress everywhere: checking inside all his shirt pockets, bending down to glance under his chair, and searching near the window sill. Seeing him so anxious, I walked up close to him with a giggle and said, "Stop searching, Papa! Look, your spectacles are sitting right there on your head!" Papa burst into laughter, and everyone in the family smiled.

🔥 Page 9: Matchstick Puzzle & Optical Illusion

A. Move 3 matchsticks to make 3 squares
Solution: The puzzle starts with 4 or 5 connected matchstick squares. By taking 3 protruding matchsticks from the outer corners and placing them into open gaps, you complete exactly 3 congruent, connected square boundaries with zero wasted matches!
B. Look at the flame (Touch red star with nose tip)
Scientific Secret: When the tip of your nose touches the page, both your eyes must cross and converge very close. At this focal distance, the separate images of the candle and the flame overlap on the retinas of your eyes, creating the amazing optical illusion that the candle has started burning!

🔍 Page 10: Find 'd' & Fun Riddle

C. Look carefully. Can you find 'd'?
Exact Location: Look at Row 4, Column 2! In a grid filled with 'b' letters, the second letter in the fourth row is 'd'!
Riddle: "I go to the market, I lovingly bring you home, I sit in front of you, And I cry, cry, cry. What is it?"
Answer: 🧅 An Onion (प्याज़)!
Why? When you buy onions from the vegetable vendor and chop them in the kitchen, their sulfur compounds vaporize and irritate your tear glands, making you cry!
