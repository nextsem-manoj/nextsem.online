Exercise 6.2 — Q1

Problem Statement

A square loop of side 10 cm and resistance 0.5 Ω is placed vertically in the east-west plane. A uniform magnetic field of 0.10 T is set up across the plane in the north-east direction. Determine the induced current.

functions Governing Relations SI Units
Φ = B · A cos θ
Ε = - ΔΦ / Δt
I = Ε / R

format_list_numbered Derivation Steps

Step 1: Compute Loop Area (A) Geometry

Side of square l = 10 cm = 0.10 m:

A = (0.10 m)² = 0.01 m² = 10−² m²
Step 2: Determine Orientation Angle (θ) Vector Normal

Vertical east-west plane normal points North. Field acts North-East:

θ = 45° &implies cos 45° = 1 / √2 ≈ 0.7071
Step 3: Initial Magnetic Flux Linkage Field Flux
Φ = (0.10 T) × (10−² m²) × 0.707 = 7.07 × 10−&sup4 Wb
verified 100% Verified Answer
NCERT Master Key
I = 1.06 mA (Direction: East to West)

By Lenz's Law, the polarity of induced EMF creates an opposing flux counteracting the flux drop. With the field falling to zero, the induced current produces magnetic lines directed along North-East, enforcing current travel from East to West along the topmost segment.

warning Common Mistake: Forgetting normal vector projection angle θ = 45°.
