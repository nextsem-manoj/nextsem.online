Complete practical workflow: 10 Business Transactions → Journal Entries → 12 T-Shape Ledger Accounts → Trial Balance.

1 The 10 Business Transactions (जनवरी 2026 के 10 व्यावसायिक लेन-देन)

Owner: Rahul Traders
Jan 1: Started business with Cash ₹1,00,000 (नकद पूँजी से व्यापार शुरू किया).
Jan 2: Opened a Bank account by depositing Cash ₹60,000 (बैंक खाता खोला).
Jan 4: Purchased goods for Cash ₹15,000 (नकद माल खरीदा).
Jan 7: Purchased goods on credit from Rohit ₹25,000 (रोहित से उधार माल खरीदा).
Jan 10: Sold goods for Cash ₹18,000 (नकद माल बेचा).
Jan 14: Sold goods to Ankit on credit ₹20,000 (अंकित को उधार माल बेचा).
Jan 18: Returned damaged goods to Rohit ₹3,000 (रोहित को माल वापस किया).
Jan 22: Received Cash from Ankit ₹19,500 in full settlement of ₹20,000 (Discount allowed ₹500).
Jan 26: Paid Cash to Rohit ₹21,500 in full settlement of remaining ₹22,000 (Discount received ₹500).
Jan 31: Paid Shop Rent by Cheque ₹4,000 and Salaries in Cash ₹6,000.
