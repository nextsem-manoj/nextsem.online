Speed / चाल
v = s / t
SI Unit: m/s (or km/h)
Average Speed / औसत चाल
v_avg = Total s / Total t
Equal dist: 2v₁v₂ / (v₁+v₂)
Acceleration / त्वरण
a = (v - u) / t
SI Unit: m/s²
Newton's 2nd Law / बल
F = m × a = (mv - mu)/t
SI Unit: Newton (N = kg·m/s²)
1st Equation / प्रथम समीकरण
v = u + a·t
Velocity-Time Relation
2nd Equation / द्वितीय समीकरण
s = u·t + ½·a·t²
Position-Time Relation
3rd Equation / तृतीय समीकरण
v² - u² = 2·a·s
Position-Velocity Relation
Unit Conversion / इकाई रूपांतरण
