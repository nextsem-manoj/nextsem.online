Step-by-Step Exemplar Derivation NCERT Eq. 6.22
E = -dΦ/dt = N · B · A · ω · sin(ωt) ⟹ E₀ = N · B · A · ω

1. Area of circular coil: A = π · r² = 3.1416 × (0.08)² = 2.01 × 10⁻² m²

2. Maximum value condition: sin(ωt) = 1 (normal to field axis)

3. Direct Substitution:

E₀ = 20 × (3.0 × 10⁻² T) × (2.01 × 10⁻² m²) × (50 rad/s)

E₀ = 20 × 0.03 × 0.0201 × 50 = 0.603 V
