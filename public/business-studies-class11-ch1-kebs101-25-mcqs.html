Q1 of 25 History of Trade & Indigenous Banking
📖 PDF Page 3

In ancient and medieval India, which credit instrument was widely used by merchant communities for carrying out transactions involving transfer of money from one person or place to another without physical movement of cash?

✓ Verified NCERT Explanation

According to NCERT Chapter 1 (Page 3-4), Hundi and Chitti were indigenous credit instruments used in ancient India. The word 'Hundi' is derived from the Sanskrit word meaning 'to collect'. It was a contract warranting payment of money, capable of being negotiated by endorsement.

Q2 of 25 Types of Hundis (Jokhmi Hundi)
📖 PDF Page 4

Which specific type of ancient Hundi was drawn against dispatched goods, where if the goods were lost in transit due to shipwreck or perils of the sea, the drawer or holder bore the loss, functioning as an early form of marine insurance?

✓ Verified NCERT Explanation

NCERT Table 1.1 (Page 5) classifies 'Jokhmi Hundi' as a Muddati Hundi drawn against dispatched goods. If goods were lost in transit, the drawer/holder absorbed the financial loss, effectively acting as an indigenous marine insurance contract.
