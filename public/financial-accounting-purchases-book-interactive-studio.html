1. The Three Golden Filters of Purchases Book

Which transactions enter the Purchases Day Book?

✅ RECORDED HERE
Credit Purchases of Goods
Only purchases of merchandise/inventory intended for resale on credit terms are recorded in the Purchases Book.
❌ NOT RECORDED (CASH BOOK)
Cash Purchases of Goods
Any purchase of goods for cash or cheque goes directly to the Cash Book (Payment side).
❌ NOT RECORDED (JOURNAL PROPER)
Credit Purchase of Fixed Assets
Purchases of Machinery, Furniture, Computers, or Building on credit are recorded in the Journal Proper.

5. Quick Reference & Exam Golden Points

  • Source Document: The entry in the Purchases Book is made on the basis of the Inward Invoice / Bill received from the seller.
  • Trade Discount: Trade discount is deducted directly on the face of the invoice and is never recorded separately in the books of accounts.
  • Cash Discount: Cash discount is not recorded in the Purchases Book; it is recorded in the Cash Book when the payment is made.
  • Periodic Total: The monthly/weekly total of the Purchases Book is debited to the Purchases Account with the narration "To Sundries as per Purchases Book".
