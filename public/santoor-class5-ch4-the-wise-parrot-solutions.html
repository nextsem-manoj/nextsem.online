📝 Let us Think: Comprehension Q&A

Page 45 Textbook
Q1. Why did the merchant keep the parrot in a cage of gold?
Answer: The merchant was very wealthy and loved the parrot dearly. He kept it in a cage of gold because of its sharp mind, sweet voice, and the entertaining stories and songs it shared.
Q2. What made the parrot a favourite pet of the merchant?
Answer: The parrot had a remarkably sharp and talkative mind. It constantly entertained the merchant with delightful stories, songs, and cheerful company.
Q3. What message did the parrot ask the merchant to convey to its friends?
Answer: When the merchant refused to free it, the parrot sighed and requested: "When you see my friends in the jungle, tell them that I send them greetings from my cage."
Q4. What happened after the merchant gave the message to the parrots?
Answer: As soon as the merchant conveyed the message, one wild parrot dropped like a stone from the tree branch and fell to the ground, playing dead as a secret demonstration!
Q5. How did the parrot escape from the cage?
Answer: The pet parrot grasped the secret clue immediately and feigned death in its cage. When the grieved merchant placed its limp body on the windowsill, the bird stirred, stretched its green wings, and soared away into the open sky!

💭 Think and Discuss (Critical Thinking)

Page 45 Section B
B1. If you were the merchant, how would you respond to the parrot's request for freedom?
Answer: If I were the merchant, I would realize that true love means wishing happiness for the other. Keeping a creature confined in a cage, even a golden one, is selfish. I would open the cage door and happily let it fly in the open sky.
B2. Can you think of a real-life situation where intelligence is more useful than strength?
Answer: Yes, in situations like solving a complex mechanical breakdown, resolving a heated argument peacefully, or rescuing someone trapped without causing harm, intelligence and calm strategy always triumph over mere muscle strength.
B3. Which parrot from the chapter was actually 'wise' according to you? Give reasons.
Answer: Both parrots were wise in different ways! The wild parrot was wise because it safely communicated a life-saving escape plan without uttering words that the merchant could suspect. The pet parrot was equally wise because it understood the subtle hint instantly and executed the trick with courage.

⏳ Present Perfect Tense Exercises

Subject + has/have + V3 + Object
a.I ...................... (visit) Delhi many times. It is a city with many historical sites.
Answer: I have visited Delhi many times.
b.My friend Onshangla is an artist. She ...................... (draw) pictures of ten monuments.
Answer: She has drawn pictures of ten monuments.
c.Mr. Joon ...................... (buy) a new car.
Answer: Mr. Joon has bought a new car.
d.I ...................... (complete) my homework and my brother ...................... (revise) for his test.
Answer: I have completed my homework and my brother has revised for his test.
e.They ...................... (write) letters to their friends.
Answer: They have written letters to their friends.

🥣 Let us Do: Eco-Friendly Bird Feeder Project

Page 50–51

1. Identified Materials:

  • Plastic Bottle Feeder: Clean empty bottle, 2 wooden spoons as perches, jute thread.
  • Terracotta Clay Feeder: Earthen saucer/pot, cotton macrame rope hanger.
  • Coconut Shell Feeder: Halved dry coconut shell, 2 holes pierced with strong twine.

2. Five Easy DIY Steps:

  1. Scrub and wash a natural coconut shell or recycled bottle.
  2. Pierce two holes on opposite edges with adult guidance.
  3. Thread sturdy jute rope and tie a secure knot.
  4. Fill with millets (bajra), sunflower seeds, and grains.
  5. Hang in a safe balcony spot away from stray cats!
