Q.8 (Attempt either A or B) 3 Marks

(A) Describe the time period of a simple pendulum. A simple pendulum takes 20 seconds to complete 10 oscillations. Calculate its time period.
एक सरल लोलक के आवर्तकाल का वर्णन कीजिए। एक सरल लोलक 10 दोलन पूरे करने में 20 सेकंड का समय लेता है। इसका आवर्तकाल परिकलित कीजिए।

OR / अथवा

(B) A train covers a distance of 300 km in 6 hours. Calculate its speed in km/hr. Calculate the distance covered by the train in 12 hours if it continues to move with the same speed.
एक रेलगाड़ी 6 घंटे में 300 किमी की दूरी तय करती है। इसकी चाल किमी/घंटा में ज्ञात कीजिए। यदि वह उसी चाल से चलती रहे, तो 12 घंटे में कितनी दूरी तय करेगी?

Model Solution & Marking Scheme 3 Marks

Solution for Choice (A):

1. Definition of Time Period (1 Mark): The time taken by a simple pendulum to complete one complete oscillation (from mean position to one extreme, to the other extreme, and back to mean) is defined as its time period ($T$).

2. Calculation (2 Marks):

  • Total time taken ($t$) = 20 seconds
  • Total number of oscillations ($n$) = 10
  • $\text{Time Period } (T) = \frac{\text{Total Time Taken}}{\text{Number of Oscillations}} = \frac{20\text{ s}}{10} = \mathbf{2\text{ seconds}}$

Result: The time period of the simple pendulum is 2 seconds (2 s).

Solution for Choice (B) [OR]:

Step 1: Calculate Speed of the Train (1.5 Marks):

  • Distance ($D_1$) = 300 km
  • Time ($t_1$) = 6 hours
  • $\text{Speed } (S) = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{300\text{ km}}{6\text{ h}} = \mathbf{50\text{ km/h}}$

Step 2: Calculate Distance Covered in 12 Hours (1.5 Marks):

  • Speed ($S$) = 50 km/h (constant)
  • Time ($t_2$) = 12 hours
  • $\text{Distance } (D_2) = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time} = 50\text{ km/h} \times 12\text{ h} = \mathbf{600\text{ km}}$

Result: Speed = 50 km/h, Distance in 12 hours = 600 km.

🎯 Marking Breakdown: 1.5 Marks for speed formula, steps & answer + 1.5 Marks for distance formula, steps & answer.

Solution for Choice (I):

Process Movement of Ribs Movement of Diaphragm Volume & Pressure Effect
