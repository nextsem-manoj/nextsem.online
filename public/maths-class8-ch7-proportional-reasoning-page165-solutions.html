Scientific & Mathematical Explanations:

1. Why is 20 mL decoction + 30 mL milk coffee stronger?
In regular coffee, the ratio of decoction to milk is $\frac{15}{35} = \frac{3}{7} \approx 0.429$ (or $30\%$ decoction in total drink).
In this coffee, the ratio is $\frac{20}{30} = \frac{2}{3} \approx 0.667$ (or $40\%$ decoction in total drink).
Since $\frac{2}{3} > \frac{3}{7}$, there is more coffee decoction per unit of milk, making it taste stronger.

2. Why is 10 mL decoction + 40 mL milk coffee lighter?
Here, the ratio is $\frac{10}{40} = \frac{1}{4} = 0.250$ (or $20\%$ decoction in total drink).
Since $\frac{1}{4} < \frac{3}{7}$ ($0.250 < 0.429$), there is less coffee decoction per unit of milk, making it taste lighter / milder.

3. Mathematical Criterion for the Table:
Compare each fraction $\frac{\text{Decoction}}{\text{Milk}}$ to the benchmark fraction $\frac{3}{7} \approx 0.4286$:

  • Row 1 ($300 : 600$): $\frac{300}{600} = \frac{1}{2} = 0.500 > \frac{3}{7} \implies$ Stronger
  • Row 2 ($150 : 500$): $\frac{150}{500} = \frac{3}{10} = 0.300 < \frac{3}{7} \implies$ Lighter
  • Row 3 ($200 : 400$): $\frac{200}{400} = \frac{1}{2} = 0.500 > \frac{3}{7} \implies$ Stronger
  • Row 4 ($24 : 56$): Dividing both by 8 gives $\frac{3}{7}$, exactly equal to benchmark $\implies$ Regular
  • Row 5 ($100 : 300$): $\frac{100}{300} = \frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333 < \frac{3}{7} \implies$ Lighter

🎯 Figure it Out — Question 1
Page 165 • True/False Proportion

1. Circle the following statements of proportion that are true.
1. नीचे दिए गए समानुपात के कथनों में से जो सत्य हैं उन पर घेरा लगाइए:

(i) 4 : 7 :: 12 : 21
✅ TRUE (सत्य)
(ii) 8 : 3 :: 24 : 6
❌ FALSE (असत्य)
(iii) 7 : 12 :: 12 : 7
❌ FALSE (असत्य)
(iv) 21 : 6 :: 35 : 10
✅ TRUE (सत्य)
(v) 12 : 18 :: 28 : 12
❌ FALSE (असत्य)
(vi) 24 : 8 :: 9 : 3
✅ TRUE (सत्य)

Proportion Rule:

Four terms $a : b :: c : d$ are in proportion if and only if the product of extremes equals the product of means:

a \times d = b \times c \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}
  1. (i) $4 : 7 :: 12 : 21$:
    Product of extremes = $4 \times 21 = 84$
    Product of means = $7 \times 12 = 84$
    Since $84 = 84$, this statement is TRUE.
  2. (ii) $8 : 3 :: 24 : 6$:
    Product of extremes = $8 \times 6 = 48$
    Product of means = $3 \times 24 = 72$
    Since $48 \neq 72$, this statement is FALSE.
  3. (iii) $7 : 12 :: 12 : 7$:
    Product of extremes = $7 \times 7 = 49$
    Product of means = $12 \times 12 = 144$
    Since $49 \neq 144$, this statement is FALSE.
  4. (iv) $21 : 6 :: 35 : 10$:
    Product of extremes = $21 \times 10 = 210$
    Product of means = $6 \times 35 = 210$
    Since $210 = 210$, this statement is TRUE. (Also $\frac{21}{6} = \frac{7}{2}$ and $\frac{35}{10} = \frac{7}{2}$).
  5. (v) $12 : 18 :: 28 : 12$:
    Product of extremes = $12 \times 12 = 144$
    Product of means = $18 \times 28 = 504$
    Since $144 \neq 504$, this statement is FALSE.
  6. (vi) $24 : 8 :: 9 : 3$:
    Product of extremes = $24 \times 3 = 72$
    Product of means = $8 \times 9 = 72$
    Since $72 = 72$, this statement is TRUE. (Both reduce to $3 : 1$).

🎉 Official Textbook Conclusion: Statements (i), (iv), and (vi) are circled.

🎯 Figure it Out — Question 2
Page 165 • Equivalent Ratios

2. Give 3 ratios that are proportional to 4 : 9.
2. 4 : 9 के समानुपाती 3 अनुपात दीजिए:

Ratio 1: 8 : 18
Ratio 2: 12 : 27
Ratio 3: 16 : 36

Derivation Method:

To find proportional ratios, multiply both terms of the ratio $\frac{4}{9}$ by the same non-zero integer $k$:

  • For $k = 2$: $\frac{4 \times 2}{9 \times 2} = \frac{8}{18} \implies \mathbf{8 : 18}$
  • For $k = 3$: $\frac{4 \times 3}{9 \times 3} = \frac{12}{27} \implies \mathbf{12 : 27}$
  • For $k = 4$: $\frac{4 \times 4}{9 \times 4} = \frac{16}{36} \implies \mathbf{16 : 36}$

Other valid examples: $20 : 45$ (for $k=5$), $40 : 90$ (for $k=10$), etc.

🎯 Figure it Out — Question 3
Page 165 • Missing Term Proportions

3. Fill in the missing numbers for these ratios that are proportional to 18 : 24.
3. 18 : 24 के समानुपाती इन अनुपातों के लिए लुप्त संख्याएँ भरिए:

Part (i)
3 : 4
Part (ii)
12 : 16
Part (iii)
20 : 80/3 (26⅔)
Part (iv)
27 : 36

Step-by-Step Mathematical Solutions:

First, reduce the base ratio to its simplest form:

\frac{18}{24} = \frac{18 \div 6}{24 \div 6} = \frac{3}{4} \quad (\text{HCF of } 18 \text{ and } 24 \text{ is } 6)
  1. (i) $3 : \underline{\quad}$
    Let the missing number be $x$.
    $$\frac{18}{24} = \frac{3}{x} \implies \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3}{x} \implies x = \mathbf{4}$$ Ratio is $3 : 4$.
  2. (ii) $12 : \underline{\quad}$
    Let the missing number be $y$.
    $$\frac{3}{4} = \frac{12}{y} \implies 3y = 4 \times 12 = 48 \implies y = \frac{48}{3} = \mathbf{16}$$ Ratio is $12 : 16$.
  3. (iii) $20 : \underline{\quad}$
    Let the missing number be $z$.
    $$\frac{3}{4} = \frac{20}{z} \implies 3z = 4 \times 20 = 80 \implies z = \mathbf{\frac{80}{3}} \approx 26.67 \text{ (or } 26\frac{2}{3}\text{)}$$ Ratio is $20 : \frac{80}{3}$ (Matches the official textbook solution key).
  4. (iv) $27 : \underline{\quad}$
    Let the missing number be $w$.
    $$\frac{3}{4} = \frac{27}{w} \implies 3w = 4 \times 27 = 108 \implies w = \frac{108}{3} = \mathbf{36}$$ Ratio is $27 : 36$.
