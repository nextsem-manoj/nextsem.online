🏛️ Part 2: Complete Catalog of Circle Theorems (Class 9 & 10)
Theorem 1 SSS Congruence
Equal Chords Subtend Equal Angles at the Center
If two chords of a circle are equal in length ($AB = CD$), then they subtend equal angles at the center ($\angle AOB = \angle COD$).
Proof: $\Delta AOB \cong \Delta COD$ by SSS ($OA=OC, OB=OD$ radii, $AB=CD$). Hence $\angle AOB = \angle COD$ (CPCTC).
Theorem 3 RHS Congruence
Perpendicular from Center to Chord Bisects the Chord
The perpendicular drawn from the center of a circle to a chord bisects the chord ($OM \perp AB \implies AM = MB = \frac{1}{2}AB$).
Pythagorean Link: In right $\Delta OMA$: $r^2 = d^2 + (\text{chord}/2)^2$.
Theorem 8 (Master Theorem) $\angle AOB = 2\angle APB$
Central Angle is Double the Inscribed Angle
The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle.
Corollary 1: Angle in a semicircle is a right angle ($90^\circ$).
Corollary 2: Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal ($\angle APB = \angle AQB$).
Theorem 11 Cyclic Quadrilaterals
Opposite Angles of a Cyclic Quadrilateral are Supplementary
The sum of either pair of opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral is $180^\circ$ ($\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ$ and $\angle B + \angle D = 180^\circ$).
Exterior Angle Property: If one side is produced, Exterior Angle = Interior Opposite Angle.
Theorems 14 & 15 (Tangents) Class 10 Core
Tangent Perpendicularity & External Tangents Equality
1. Radius through point of contact is perpendicular to tangent ($OP \perp XY$).
2. Lengths of two tangents drawn from an external point are equal ($PA = PB$).
Proof: $\Delta OAP \cong \Delta OBP$ by RHS $\implies PA = PB$ and $OP$ bisects $\angle APB$.
