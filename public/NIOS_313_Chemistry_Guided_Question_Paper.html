💡 Hint: \(\Delta H_{\text{rxn}} = \sum \text{BE}(\text{bonds broken}) - \sum \text{BE}(\text{bonds formed})\). Note that each \(\text{H}_2\text{O}\) has two \(\text{O}-\text{H}\) bonds.

- Bonds formed: In \(2\text{H}_2\text{O}\), there are \(2 \times 2 = 4\) moles of \(\text{O}-\text{H}\) bonds.
Energy released \(= 4 \times 423 = 1692\text{ kJ}\).

📝 Your Turn:

Compute \(\Delta H_{\text{rxn}}\): $$\Delta H_{\text{rxn}} = 1368.4\text{ kJ} - \text{energy formed} = \mathbf{\text{calculated value}}\text{ kJ}$$ Since \(\Delta H < 0\), the reaction is exothermic / endothermic.

  2. \(\text{CCl}_4\) does not hydrolyse but \(\text{SiCl}_4\) does.
  3. \(\text{N}_2\) is chemically inert at room temperature.
  4. \(\text{SF}_6\) is known, but \(\text{SCl}_6\) is not.

📝 Your Turn (Complete Parts 3 & 4):

Complete for \(\text{SiCl}_4\): Silicon has accessible vacant d-orbitals which permit coordination with water.
3. \(\text{N}_2\) inertness: The \(\text{N}\equiv\text{N}\) triple bond has a very high bond dissociation enthalpy of 946 kJ/mol, making it unreactive at room temperature.

💡 Hint: Primary = physical settlement; Secondary = biological microbial decomposition; Tertiary = chemical disinfection (chlorination/ozonation).

✍️ Teacher's Foundation:

📝 Your Turn (Describe Tertiary Treatment):
