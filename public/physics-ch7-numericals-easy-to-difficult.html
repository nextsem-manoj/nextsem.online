NCERT Exercise 7.2 (a) Calculating RMS Voltage from Peak Voltage
The peak voltage of an AC supply is 300 V. What is the root mean square (rms) voltage?
📐 Formula: V_rms = V_m / √2 = 0.707 × V_m
Answer: 2 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) V_rms = 300 / √2 = 300 / 1.414 = 212.1 V
👁️ Answer 212.1 V
NCERT Exercise 7.2 (b) Calculating Peak Current from RMS Current
The rms value of current in an AC circuit is 10 A. What is the peak current amplitude in the circuit?
📐 Formula: i_m = √2 × I_rms = 1.414 × I_rms
Answer: 1 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) i_m = 1.414 × 10 A = 14.14 A
👁️ Answer 14.14 A
NCERT Exercise 7.1 (a) RMS Current in a Pure Resistor Circuit

📐 Formula: I_rms = V_rms / R
Answer: 2 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) I = 220 V / 100 Ω = 2.2 A
👁️ Answer 2.2 A
NCERT Exercise 7.1 (b) Net Power Consumed Over a Full Cycle in a Resistor
For the 100 Ω resistor connected to the 220 V, 50 Hz supply, what is the net power consumed over a complete cycle?
📐 Formula: P = V_rms × I_rms = I_rms² × R = V²/R

NCERT Exercise 7.6 Angular Frequency of Free LC Oscillations

NCERT Example 7.8 (a) Reactances and Total Impedance of a Series LCR Circuit
A sinusoidal peak voltage vm = 283 V at 50 Hz is applied to a series LCR circuit with R = 3 Ω, L = 25.48 mH, and C = 796 μF. Find the impedance (Z) of the circuit:
📐 Formula: X_L = 2πfL = 8.0 Ω; X_C = 1/(2πfC) = 4.0 Ω; Z = √(R² + (X_L - X_C)²)
Answer: 5 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) X_L - X_C = 8.0 - 4.0 = 4.0 Ω; Z = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5.0 Ω
👁️ Answer 5.0 Ω
NCERT Example 7.8 (b) Phase Angle Between Source Voltage and Current
In the series LCR circuit (R = 3 Ω, XL - XC = 4 Ω), determine the phase difference (φ) between the source voltage and circuit current:
📐 Formula: tan(φ) = (X_L - X_C) / R
Answer: 5 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) tan(φ) = 4 / 3 = 1.333; φ = tan⁻¹(1.333) = 53.1° (0.927 rad); Current lags voltage by 53.1°
👁️ Answer 53.1° (0.927 rad)
NCERT Example 7.8 (c) Peak Current and Average Power Dissipated
For the series LCR circuit (vm = 283 V, Z = 5 Ω, R = 3 Ω), calculate the peak current amplitude, rms current, and total power dissipated:
📐 Formula: i_m = v_m / Z; I = i_m / √2; P = I² × R
Answer: 4 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) i_m = 283 / 5 = 56.6 A; I = 56.6 / 1.414 = 40.0 A; P = (40)² × 3 = 1600 × 3 = 4800 W
👁️ Answer 4800 W (4.8 kW)
NCERT Example 7.8 (d) Power Factor of the Series LCR Circuit
Calculate the power factor (cos φ) of the above series LCR circuit (R = 3 Ω, Z = 5 Ω):
📐 Formula: cos(φ) = R / Z
Answer: 0 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) cos(φ) = 3 Ω / 5 Ω = 0.6 (or cos(53.1°) = 0.60)
👁️ Answer 0.6

NCERT Example 7.9 (a) Resonant Frequency of Variable Frequency Source
If the frequency of the source in the LCR circuit (L = 25.48 mH, C = 796 μF) can be varied, determine the angular and linear frequency at which resonance occurs:
📐 Formula: ω_0 = 1 / √(L C); f_0 = ω_0 / (2π)
Answer: 2 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) L C = (25.48 × 10⁻³) × (796 × 10⁻⁶) = 2.028 × 10⁻⁵; ω_0 = 1 / √(2.028 × 10⁻⁵) = 222.1 rad/s; f_0 = 222.1 / (2 × 3.1416) = 35.4 Hz
👁️ Answer 222.1 rad/s (35.4 Hz)
NCERT Example 7.9 (b) Impedance, Peak Current, and Power at Resonant Condition
At the resonant condition (vm = 283 V, R = 3 Ω): calculate the impedance Z, peak current amplitude im, and power dissipated in the circuit:
📐 Formula: Z_res = R; i_m = v_m / R; I = i_m / √2; P_res = I² × R
Answer: 1 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) Z = 3 Ω; i_m = 283 / 3 = 94.33 A; I = 94.33 / 1.414 = 66.7 A; P = (66.7)² × 3 = 4451 × 3 = 13350 W = 13.35 kW
👁️ Answer Z = 3 Ω, im = 94.3 A, P = 13.35 kW
NCERT Exercise 7.7 Average Power Transferred at Natural Frequency
A series LCR circuit with R = 20 Ω, L = 1.5 H, and C = 35 μF is connected to a 200 V AC supply. When frequency equals the natural frequency, what is the average power transferred per cycle?
📐 Formula: At resonance, Z = R = 20 Ω; I = V / R; P = V² / R
Answer: 2 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) I = 200 V / 20 Ω = 10 A; P = (200)² / 20 = 40000 / 20 = 2000 W = 2 kW
👁️ Answer 2000 W (2 kW)
NCERT Exercise 7.8 (a) Resonant Angular Frequency for L = 5.0 H, C = 80 μF
A series LCR circuit connected to a 230 V source has L = 5.0 H, C = 80 μF, R = 40 Ω. Determine the source frequency (ω0) which drives the circuit into resonance:

NCERT Exercise 7.8 (c) RMS Potential Drops and Zero Potential Across LC Combination
Determine the rms potential drops across R, L, and C (VR, VL, VC). What is the net potential drop across the LC series combination at resonance?

NCERT Textbook Section 7.9 Secondary Current in a Step-Down Transformer
An ideal transformer steps down voltage from 2200 V to 220 V. If the primary winding carries a current of 5 A, what is the output secondary current?
📐 Formula: V_s / V_p = I_p / I_s => I_s = I_p × (V_p / V_s)
Answer: 5 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) V_p / V_s = 2200 / 220 = 10; I_s = 5 A × 10 = 50 A
👁️ Answer 50 A
NCERT Textbook Section 7.9 Turns Ratio for a Step-Up Grid Transformer
Electric power generated at 220 V is stepped up to 22,000 V for long-distance grid transmission. What turns ratio (Ns / Np) must the transformer possess?
📐 Formula: N_s / N_p = V_s / V_p
Answer: 1 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) N_s / N_p = 22000 V / 220 V = 100
👁️ Answer 100 (Turns Ratio 100 : 1)
NCERT Textbook Section 7.9 (Transmission) Transmission Joule Heat Loss Reduction Factor
If transmission voltage is stepped up by a factor of 100 (N = 100), by what factor is the Joule heating energy loss (I²R) in the transmission cables reduced?

Answer: 1 _______________
💡 Hint (Formula & Steps) Power loss reduction = (1 / 100)² = 1 / 10,000 (Loss is reduced by a factor of 10,000)
👁️ Answer 10,000 times (Reduced by 10⁴)
