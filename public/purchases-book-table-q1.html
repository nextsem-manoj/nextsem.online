🧮 2. Step-by-Step Calculation Breakdown
1. Gross Amount: (20 × ₹30,000) + (15 × ₹8,000) = ₹6,00,000 + ₹1,20,000 = ₹7,20,000
2. Trade Discount: 10% of ₹7,20,000 = ₹72,000 (Deducted before tax; not shown in ledger)
3. Taxable Net Cost: ₹7,20,000 - ₹72,000 = ₹6,48,000 (Transferred to Purchases A/c Dr.)
4. Input GST: CGST 9% (₹58,320) + SGST 9% (₹58,320) = ₹1,16,640 (Asset accounts Dr.)
5. Total Payable: ₹6,48,000 + ₹58,320 + ₹58,320 = ₹7,64,640 (Credited to M/s Modern Electronics)
