NCERT Example 1 • Transaction Impact Analysis

Dual Aspect Impact on Assets & Liabilities

📋 Transaction Set (from Textbook Example 1):

# Transaction Details Asset / Liability Affected Accounting Effect
1 Opened a bank account in State Bank of India with an amount of ₹4,80,000. Bank (Asset) & Cash (Asset) Bank +₹4,80,000, Cash -₹4,80,000
2 Bought furniture for ₹60,000 and cheque was issued on the same day. Furniture (Asset) & Bank (Asset) Furniture +₹60,000, Bank -₹60,000
3 Bought plant and machinery for ₹1,25,000; paid advance of ₹10,000 cash to M/s Ramjee Lal. Plant (Asset), Cash (Asset) & Creditor Ramjee Lal (Liability) Plant +₹1,25,000, Cash -₹10,000, Creditors +₹1,15,000
✏️ Test Calculations (Strict Mode — No Hints)

1. In Transaction 1, the total net change in total assets of the firm is exactly (1).

👁️ Check Solution₹0 [Zero net change: Bank increases by ₹4,80,000 and Cash decreases by ₹4,80,000]

2. In Transaction 2, after issuing the cheque of ₹60,000, the remaining balance in the SBI Bank Account is (2).
👁️ Check Solution₹4,20,000 [₹4,80,000 - ₹60,000 = ₹4,20,000]

3. In Transaction 3, the liability created under Creditors (M/s Ramjee Lal) is (3).
👁️ Check Solution₹1,15,000 [₹1,25,000 total plant cost - ₹10,000 cash advance = ₹1,15,000]

4. Combining all 3 transactions, the net increase in Total Assets ($A$) is exactly equal to the increase in Total Liabilities ($L$) which is (4).
👁️ Check Solution₹1,15,000

🧩 Match the Following Transaction Effects
A Open bank account with ₹4,80,000 cash
B Bought furniture ₹60,000 by cheque
C Plant ₹1,25,000 with ₹10,000 cash advance
D Net effect of depositing cash into bank
E Credit obligation remaining on machinery
