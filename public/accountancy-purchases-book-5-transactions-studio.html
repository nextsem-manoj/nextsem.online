⚠️ Part 4: Purchases Book Golden Rules & Exam Traps
✅ What is RECORDED:

ONLY Credit purchases of GOODS (merchandise meant for resale or core production).

❌ Cash Purchases EXCLUDED:

Any purchase paid immediately by Cash/Bank is recorded directly in the Cash Book.

❌ Asset Purchases EXCLUDED:

Credit purchase of capital assets (Furniture, Machinery, Computers for office use) goes to Journal Proper.

💡 Trade Discount Rule:

Deducted immediately from gross invoice value. GST is applied only on the net discounted cost.
