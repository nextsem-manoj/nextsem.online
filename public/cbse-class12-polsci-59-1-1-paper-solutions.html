Given below are two statements :
Statement-I : The economic policies implemented by China helped to break the stagnation of its economy.
Statement-II : Economic development of China also increased environmental degradation and corruption.

उपर्युक्त कथनों के संदर्भ में, नीचे दिए गए विकल्पों में से सही उत्तर चुनिए :

Correct Answer: (A) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are true.

Explanation:
• Statement-I is TRUE: China's new economic policies introduced by Deng Xiaoping (Open Door Policy in 1978, privatization of agriculture in 1982, privatization of industry in 1998, creation of Special Economic Zones) helped break the chronic stagnation of the command economy and produced phenomenal economic growth.

✓ Official Solution & Rationale

Two Traditional Measures for National Security (External Security):
  1. Balance of Power:

    When countries recognize that a neighbouring or rival state is becoming militarily or economically superior, they perceive a looming security threat. To prevent aggression, states actively maintain a "balance of power" by upgrading modern weaponry, expanding armed forces, strengthening border fortifications, and accelerating domestic economic and technological capabilities so that no adversary can risk an attack.

  2. Alliance Building:

    An alliance is a formal treaty-based coalition of sovereign nations formed to deter or mutually defend against armed military aggression. Smaller or medium nations form alliances with superpowers or regional partners (e.g., NATO, Warsaw Pact) to combine their collective defense capabilities. Alliances deter prospective aggressors by demonstrating that an attack on one member will be treated as an attack on all members.

Solution to Part (a): Four Factors Responsible for the Rise of Coalition Governments (Post-1989):
  1. Decline of One-Party Dominance (End of Congress System): The 1989 elections ended the era of single-party Congress dominance. No single national party won a clear parliamentary majority (272 seats), making multi-party alliances essential to form a stable government.
  2. Ascendance of Regional Parties: Strong state-level regional parties (TDP, AIADMK, DMK, SP, BSP, RJD, BJD, Trinamool) emerged to champion local and linguistic interests. They became kingmakers at the Centre, as neither Congress nor BJP could form a government without their support.
  3. The Mandal Issue and Rise of Backward Caste Politics: The implementation of the Mandal Commission recommendations (27% OBC reservation in central jobs) mobilized Other Backward Classes (OBCs) socially and politically, giving birth to assertive non-Congress, non-BJP regional parties representing marginalized sections.
  4. Rise of Cultural Nationalism and Multi-Polar Competition: The growth of the BJP around identity and cultural nationalism created a multipolar political spectrum, dividing votes among Congress, BJP, Left, and regional fronts, necessitating broad pre-poll or post-poll coalitions (like National Front, United Front, NDA, UPA).
Solution to Part (b) (OR): Four Issues of Broad Consensus Among Political Parties:
  1. Consensus on New Economic Policies: Across ideological divides, major parties agree on economic liberalization, deregulation, integration with the global economy, and modern infrastructure development. Successive coalition governments have continued these policies.
  2. Acceptance of Political and Social Claims of Backward Classes: There is complete consensus that reservation and affirmative action for Other Backward Classes (OBCs) in education and government employment must be supported and protected.
  3. Acceptance of the Role of State-Level Parties in National Governance: The distinction between state-level and national parties has blurred. All parties now accept coalition governance and power-sharing with regional parties as an enduring federal reality.
  4. Pragmatic Politics Over Dogmatic Ideology: Political competition has shifted from rigid ideological dogmatism to pragmatic, programmatic governance and coalition compromises based on Common Minimum Programmes.
