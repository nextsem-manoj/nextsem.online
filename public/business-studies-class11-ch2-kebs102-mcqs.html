Q4 of 25 Joint Hindu Family Business: Karta & Coparceners
📖 PDF Page 31

In a Joint Hindu Family Business governed by Hindu Law, what is the nature of the liability of the 'Karta' versus the other family members ('Coparceners')?

✓ Verified NCERT Explanation

NCERT Page 31 states: The liability of the Karta (the eldest male/female head) is unlimited, whereas the liability of all other members (coparceners) is strictly limited to their respective shares in the ancestral coparcenary property.

Q5 of 25 Joint Hindu Family Business: Dayabhaga vs Mitakshara
