💡 1. The Dual Perspective of Bank Transactions (दोहरा दृष्टिकोण)

Every business firm maintaining a bank account keeps an internal record in its Cash Book (Bank Column). Simultaneously, the commercial bank maintains a running account of that customer in the Bank Pass Book (or Bank Statement).

प्रत्येक व्यावसायिक फर्म बैंक लेन-देन का लेखा अपनी रोकड़ बही के बैंक कॉलम में करती है। दूसरी ओर, बैंक अपने खाताधारक का रिकॉर्ड बैंक पासबुक (या बैंक विवरण) में रखता है।
Transaction / स्थिति Cash Book (Bank Column) [व्यापारी] Bank Pass Book [बैंक की पुस्तक] Accounting Logic / लेखांकन कारण
Money Deposited (धन जमा करना) Debit (Dr.) Credit (Cr.) Firm's bank asset increases (Dr.); Bank's liability to customer increases (Cr.).
Money Withdrawn (धन निकालना) Credit (Cr.) Debit (Dr.) Firm's bank asset decreases (Cr.); Bank's liability to customer decreases (Dr.).
Positive / Favourable Balance (अनुकूल) Debit Balance (Dr.) Credit Balance (Cr.) Customer has surplus funds sitting in the bank account.
Bank Overdraft / Unfavourable (प्रतिकूल) Credit Balance (Cr.) Debit Balance (Dr.) Customer owes money to the bank under an overdraft facility.

🎯 2. Meaning & Need of Bank Reconciliation Statement (अर्थ एवं आवश्यकता)

Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS) is a statement prepared periodically (monthly, quarterly, or on 31st March) by an account holder to reconcile and explain the causes of difference between the bank balance shown in the Cash Book and the balance shown in the Pass Book as on a specific date.

बैंक समाधान विवरण एक ऐसा विवरण-पत्र है जो खाताधारक द्वारा एक निश्चित तिथि पर रोकड़ बही के बैंक कॉलम और बैंक पासबुक के शेष के बीच के अंतर को स्पष्ट करने तथा उनका मिलान करने के लिए बनाया जाता है।

📌 Salient Features of BRS (मुख्य विशेषताएँ)

  • Statement, Not an Account: It is a memorandum statement and does NOT form part of the double-entry ledger.
  • Prepared by the Customer: The business firm compiles it, NEVER the bank.
  • As on a Particular Date: Valid strictly for a specific cut-off date (e.g., "As on 31st March, 2026").
  • Ensures Accuracy: Detects uncredited cheques, delayed payments, bank charges, and clerical errors.

⏳ 3. Timing Differences (समय अंतराल संबंधी 7 प्रमुख कारण)

Timing differences arise because of the unavoidable time lag between recording a transaction in the Cash Book and its clearing/posting by the banking system.

समय अंतराल के कारण उत्पन्न अंतर तब आते हैं जब फर्म और बैंक द्वारा एक ही लेन-देन को अलग-अलग तारीखों पर दर्ज किया जाता है।
Cash Book Dr. Start: ADD (+)

1. Cheques Issued but Not Yet Presented for Payment

Firm credits Cash Book immediately. Creditor has not yet encashed it at bank. Pass Book balance remains HIGHER.

निर्गमित चेक जो अभी भुगतान हेतु प्रस्तुत नहीं हुए: रोकड़ बही कम हो चुकी है, पासबुक अभी अधिक है। (ADD)
Cash Book Dr. Start: DEDUCT (–)

2. Cheques Deposited but Not Yet Collected / Credited

Firm debits Cash Book on deposit. Clearing house takes 2-3 days to credit funds. Pass Book balance is LOWER.

जमा किए गए चेक जिनका संग्रहण नहीं हुआ: रोकड़ बही बढ़ चुकी है, पासबुक अभी कम है। (DEDUCT)
Cash Book Dr. Start: DEDUCT (–)

3. Direct Debits & Bank Charges

Bank debits service fees, SMS alerts, or overdraft interest directly. Pass Book balance is LOWER.

बैंक प्रभार या अधिविकर्ष ब्याज: बैंक ने सीधे काट लिया, पासबुक कम हो गई। (DEDUCT)
Cash Book Dr. Start: ADD (+)

4. Direct Deposits by Customers into Bank

Customer transfers funds via NEFT/UPI directly into bank account. Pass Book balance is HIGHER.

ग्राहकों द्वारा सीधे बैंक खाते में जमा: बैंक में पैसा बढ़ गया, रोकड़ बही में पता चलने तक पासबुक अधिक रहेगी। (ADD)
Cash Book Dr. Start: ADD (+)

5. Interest & Dividends Collected by Bank

Bank collects investment dividends and credits account under authority. Pass Book balance is HIGHER.

बैंक द्वारा सीधे लाभांश या ब्याज वसूलना: पासबुक में जमा हो गया, पासबुक अधिक रहेगी। (ADD)
Cash Book Dr. Start: DEDUCT (–)

6. Standing Order Direct Payments by Bank

Bank automatically pays insurance premium, EMI, or rent as instructed. Pass Book balance is LOWER.

स्थायी आदेश पर बैंक द्वारा भुगतान (बीमा प्रीमियम/किराया): पासबुक कम हो गई। (DEDUCT)
Cash Book Dr. Start: DEDUCT (–)

7. Dishonour of Cheques Deposited / Bills Discounted

Deposited cheque bounces; bank immediately debits customer's account. Pass Book balance is LOWER.

जमा किया गया चेक अनादरित होना: बैंक ने राशि वापस काट ली, पासबुक कम हो गई। (DEDUCT)

⚠️ 4. Differences Caused by Errors (त्रुटियों के कारण अंतर)

(A) Errors by the Firm in Cash Book (व्यापारी द्वारा त्रुटियाँ)

  • Omission: Cheque received or issued completely omitted.
  • Wrong Amount: Cheque of ₹5,400 entered as ₹4,500.
  • Wrong Column: Cheque recorded in cash column instead of bank column.
  • Side Reversal: Cheque issued recorded on debit (receipt) side.
  • Casting Errors: Undercasting or overcasting of total columns.

(B) Errors by the Bank in Pass Book (बैंक द्वारा त्रुटियाँ)

  • Wrong Credit: Bank credits our account for deposit made by another firm with a similar name.
  • Wrong Debit: Bank debits our account for a cheque drawn by someone else.
  • Duplication: A debit or credit entry posted twice by the bank.

✏️ 6. Preparation of Amended (Adjusted) Cash Book (संशोधित रोकड़ बही)

At financial year-end, standard accounting standards require that the Cash Book be adjusted for authentic omissions and firm errors before preparing the final BRS.

✔ ENTER IN AMENDED CASH BOOK (रोकड़ बही में समायोजन)

  • Bank charges & interest debited by bank
  • Dividends & interest collected by bank
  • Direct deposits made by customers
  • Standing instruction payments made by bank
  • Dishonoured cheques and bills
  • All clerical errors made by the firm in the Cash Book

✖ ENTER ONLY IN BRS (केवल BRS में दर्ज होगा)

  • Cheques issued but not yet presented (Bank time delay)
  • Cheques deposited but not yet cleared (Bank time delay)
  • Any errors committed by the Bank in the Pass Book

🔢 8. Ten Practice Problems with Hints & Solutions (10 अभ्यास प्रश्न)

Q1. Unpresented & Uncollected Cheques (Cash Book Dr. ₹15,000) Click for Hint & Sol ▾

Problem: Cash Book debit balance ₹15,000. Cheques issued ₹4,500, presented ₹2,800. Cheques deposited ₹6,000, credited ₹3,500.

💡 Hint: Unpresented = ₹4,500 - ₹2,800 = ₹1,700 (+). Uncollected = ₹6,000 - ₹3,500 = ₹2,500 (–).

Calculation: ₹15,000 + ₹1,700 - ₹2,500 = ₹14,200 (Pass Book Cr. Balance).

Q2. Bank Charges & Direct Dividends (Cash Book Dr. ₹28,000) Click for Hint & Sol ▾

Problem: Cash Book balance ₹28,000. Locker rent debited by bank ₹350. Dividend credited by bank ₹1,400.

💡 Hint: Add dividend ₹1,400; Deduct locker rent ₹350.

Calculation: ₹28,000 + ₹1,400 - ₹350 = ₹29,050 (Pass Book Cr. Balance).

Q3. Overdraft with Dishonoured Cheque (Cash Book OD ₹8,000) Click for Hint & Sol ▾

Problem: Cash Book OD ₹8,000. Cheque of ₹2,200 returned dishonoured with noting charges ₹30.

💡 Hint: Bank debits total ₹2,230, which increases the overdraft.

Calculation: Overdraft Minus column = ₹8,000 + ₹2,230 = ₹10,230 (Pass Book OD / Dr.).

Q4. Wrong Credit Given by Bank (Cash Book Dr. ₹35,000) Click for Hint & Sol ▾

Problem: Cash Book balance ₹35,000. Bank mistakenly credited ₹4,000 belonging to Sharma Bros.

💡 Hint: Wrong credit increased Pass Book balance by ₹4,000.

Calculation: ₹35,000 + ₹4,000 = ₹39,000 (Pass Book Cr. Balance).

Q5. Cash Book Columns Undercast and Overcast Click for Hint & Sol ▾

Problem: Cash Book balance ₹22,000. Payment side overcast by ₹1,500; Receipts side undercast by ₹500.

💡 Hint: Both errors caused Cash Book balance to appear artificially low! ADD both!

Calculation: ₹22,000 + ₹1,500 + ₹500 = ₹24,000 (Pass Book Cr. Balance).
