PAGE 28 • Q1
1. Explain the meaning of the word ‘bow’ used in the poem.
Final Answer: In the poem, ‘bow’ refers to the rainbow that arches across the sky after rainy showers.
• Step 1: Stanza 2 states: "But the bow that bridges heaven, And overtops the trees..."
• Step 2: Here, Christina Rossetti metaphorically describes the arching 7-colored rainbow as a majestic bridge across the sky.
PAGE 28 • Q2
2. Which bridge does the poet like more and why?
Final Answer: The poet likes the rainbow bridge more because it overtops the trees and builds a road from earth to heaven, making it far prettier than human river bridges.
• Step 1: Human river bridges are pretty ("as pretty as you please"), but limited to crossing waters on ground level.
• Step 2: The rainbow is a divine, natural bridge spanning celestial heights from earth into the sky.
PAGE 28 • Q3
3. The word ‘these’ is used for different things in stanzas one and two. What are the things for which ‘these’ has been used in each stanza?
Final Answer: In stanza 1, ‘these’ refers to boats and ships. In stanza 2, ‘these’ refers to human-made river bridges.
• Stanza 1: Clouds sailing in the sky are prettier than these (boats and ships).
• Stanza 2: The rainbow bridging heaven is prettier than these (bridges on rivers).
PAGE 28 • Q4 (TRUE OR FALSE)
4. State whether the following statements are True or False:
  • a. Ships sail on the river. → [ FALSE ] (Ships sail on the seas)
  • b. Boats sail on the sea. → [ FALSE ] (Boats sail on the rivers)
  • c. Bridges are built on the river. → [ TRUE ✓ ] ("There are bridges on the rivers")
  • d. A road is built from earth to the sky. → [ TRUE ✓ ] (The rainbow metaphorically builds this road)
PAGE 28 • SECTION B
B. Think and Discuss:
1. What are the ways in which you can cross a river?
By walking or driving across a bridge, taking a boat, ferry, or using an overhead cable ropeway.
2. Have you ever seen a rainbow in real life or in pictures? How did you feel?
Yes! Seeing a rainbow after fresh rainfall felt breathtaking, vibrant, and filled with wondrous joy.
3. If you could walk on a rainbow, where do you think it would take you?
It would lead straight into a magical cloud-top wonderland of stars and gentle breezes!
