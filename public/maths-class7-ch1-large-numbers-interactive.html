4. Complete Exercise Solutions (Figure it Out)

Click on any problem to reveal the step-by-step reasoning

Q1. 10-Digit Extremes using Digits 0 to 9 Exactly Once
Using all digits 0-9 exactly once (first digit cannot be 0), find: (a) Largest multiple of 5, (b) Smallest even number.
(a) Largest multiple of 5: Arrange descending digits with 0 at the end:
👉 9,876,543,210

(b) Smallest even number: Start with 1, then 0, followed by ascending digits ending in even digit 8:
👉 1,023,456,798
Q3. 9-Digit Swapping Invariant Property
Write a 9-digit number where exchanging any two digits always results in a strictly bigger number. How many such numbers exist?
For any swap to yield a larger number, the digits must be in strictly ascending order ($d_1 < d_2 < \dots < d_9$).
With 9 non-zero digits (1 to 9), there is only 1 unique number:
👉 123,456,789 (Total count = 1).
Q4. Strike Out 10 Digits from 20-Digit Stream
Strike out 10 digits from 12345123451234512345 so the remaining 10-digit number is as large as possible.
Strike out 1,2,3,4 from 1st block (4 digits), strike 1,2,3,4 from 2nd block (4 digits), strike 1,2 from 3rd block (2 digits). Total struck = 10 digits.
Remaining maximum number:
👉 5534512345
Q6. Finding the 1000th Digit in Continuous Number Stream
Writing numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, ... 10, 11... What is the 1000th digit written, and at which number does it occur?
• 1-digit numbers (1-9): 9 digits (Positions 1 to 9)
• 2-digit numbers (10-99): 90 × 2 = 180 digits (Positions 10 to 189)
• Total up to 99 = 189 digits.
• Remaining to reach 1000th digit = 1000 - 189 = 811 digits.
• 3-digit numbers take 3 digits each: 811 ÷ 3 = 270 with remainder 1.
• Number = 100 + 270 = 370. Remainder 1 points to the 1st digit of 370.
👉 The 1000th digit is 3, occurring in the number 370.
Q11. Coin Stack to Match Statue of Unity
How many 1 mm thick coins must be stacked to reach the height of the Statue of Unity (182 metres)?
Height = 182 m = 182 × 1000 mm = 1,82,000 mm.
Thickness of 1 coin = 1 mm.
👉 Total coins = 1,82,000 coins (1 Lakh 82 Thousand coins).
Q13. Bar-Tailed Godwit Non-Stop Flight Record
A bar-tailed godwit flew 13,560 km non-stop in 11 days from Alaska to Australia. Find daily and hourly average speed.
👉 Distance per day: 13,560 ÷ 11 ≈ 1,232.7 km/day
👉 Distance per hour: 1,232.7 ÷ 24 ≈ 51.36 km/hour

5. Matchstick / Toothpick Logic Puzzles

7-segment display logic rules

• Stick counts: 0(6), 1(2), 2(5), 3(5), 4(4), 5(5), 6(6), 7(3), 8(7), 9(6).

• Largest number with 24 sticks: Use digit '1' (2 sticks) 12 times ➔ 111,111,111,111 (Twelve 1s).
• Smallest number with 24 sticks: Maximize sticks per digit ➔ 2,288 or 6,888.
