Q3 The polarity of induced emf is found by which of the following laws?

Lenz's Law

Correct +4

verified Explanation Verified

Lenz's Law states that the polarity of induced emf is such that it tends to produce a current which opposes the change in magnetic flux that produces it. This is a direct consequence of the Conservation of Energy.

लेन्ज़ का नियम ऊर्जा संरक्षण के सिद्धांत पर आधारित है और प्रेरित धारा की दिशा बताता है, जो फ्लक्स परिवर्तन का विरोध करती है।
